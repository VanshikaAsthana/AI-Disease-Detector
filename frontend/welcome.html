Features
Everything you need for
advanced diagnostics

A complete AI diagnostic system combining image analysis, symptom tracking, and clinical reasoning.

🩻
Medical image analysis
Upload X-rays, MRIs, CT scans, or skin photos. The AI visually analyzes the image alongside your symptoms for a combined diagnosis.
🔬
Probability comparison
Get 5 ranked diagnoses with probability scores, severity levels, and key indicators — not just a single guess.
🧬
Lab report analysis
Upload blood test results or lab reports. The AI reads and interprets abnormal values to detect conditions like diabetes or anemia.
⚡
Instant results
Full clinical reports generated in seconds — including detected abnormalities, risk factors, recommended tests, and treatment suggestions.
🔒
Fully private
Everything runs locally on your machine using Ollama. Your medical data never leaves your computer.
🚨
Urgency detection
Automatically flags emergency conditions and tells you whether to seek care immediately, within 48 hours, or routinely.
